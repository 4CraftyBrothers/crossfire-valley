Crossfire Valley — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Crossfire Valley is a single-player and local-multiplayer strategy game. It is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no servers of its own. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your campaign progress, medals, settings (such as sound), skirmish preferences, the map you are editing, and an autosave of the game in progress are stored in the app's local storage on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Play-by-link matches

The optional "Online — share link" mode does not use a server. When you finish a turn, the game encodes the moves of that turn into a link that you choose to send to your opponent through any messaging app you like. The link contains only the game's board state and moves — no names, identifiers, or device information. We never see it.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children

Because the game collects no data, it does not knowingly collect any information from children either.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data (for example, optional online play with accounts), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent where required.
